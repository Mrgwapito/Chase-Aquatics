Last updated: 2025-10-17

Payments

These terms apply to all orders placed on the Chase Aquatics website, social channels, or other official sales channels.

Accepted Methods

  • GCash and major e-wallets, bank transfer/deposit, and Cash on Delivery (COD) where available.
  • All amounts are in Philippine Peso (₱) unless stated otherwise. Prices may change without prior notice, but we honor the price shown at checkout for confirmed orders.

Order Acceptance & Invoicing

  • Your order is an offer to buy; acceptance occurs when we send an order confirmation or dispatch notice.
  • We issue an electronic invoice/OR upon payment confirmation. Please ensure accurate billing information.

Payment Timing & Verification

  • GCash/Bank: pay within the stated window in your checkout screen. Unpaid orders may be cancelled automatically.
  • We may request proof of payment or valid ID to verify high-value orders or prevent fraud (consistent with RA 8792 – E-Commerce Act).

Taxes, Fees & Pricing Errors

  • Prices are VAT-inclusive unless stated. Shipping and special handling fees are shown at checkout.
  • If a product is listed with an obvious error (e.g., ₱1 due to a typo), we may cancel and refund per good-faith error correction under the Civil Code and DTI rules.

Refunds, Chargebacks & Disputes

  • Approved refunds are processed to the original method when possible; otherwise via bank transfer/e-wallet within 7–15 business days.
  • If you initiate a chargeback without first contacting us, we may suspend services while the dispute is pending.

Compliance

  • We follow the Consumer Act of the Philippines (RA 7394) for fair trade practices and warranties, and Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas and payment-provider rules for e-payments.

Data Privacy

We process personal data in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and its IRR, and applicable National Privacy Commission (NPC) circulars.

What We Collect

  • Identity & Contact: name, address, email, phone.
  • Order & Payment: items purchased, amounts, payment references (we do not store full card details).
  • Technical: IP address, device/browser info, cookies necessary for site operation, analytics, and fraud prevention.

How We Use Data (Lawful Bases)

  • Contract: to take and fulfill your orders; customer service and delivery.
  • Legal obligation: invoicing, tax and audit records.
  • Legitimate interests: service improvement, security, fraud prevention, limited direct marketing (you may opt out).
  • Consent: optional newsletters, cookies beyond strictly necessary.

Sharing & Transfers

  • Service providers (couriers, payment gateways, IT/hosting, analytics) bound by confidentiality and data-processing agreements.
  • Authorities when required by law or valid process. We do not sell personal data.

Retention

  • Transactional and tax records: generally up to 10 years or as required by law.
  • Marketing data: until you unsubscribe or we no longer need it.

Your Rights (RA 10173)

  • Right to be informed, access, object, erasure/blocking, damages, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.
  • To exercise rights, email our Data Protection Officer at privacy@chaseaquatics.ph. We may verify your identity.

Security

  • We use reasonable and appropriate organizational, physical, and technical measures (encryption in transit, role-based access, logging).
  • No method is 100% secure; report incidents to the DPO immediately.

Cookies

  • Essential cookies run by default. Analytics/marketing cookies run with your consent. Manage preferences via your browser or our cookie banner (if enabled).

Shipping, Returns & Warranty

Shipping

  • We ship nationwide via accredited third-party couriers.
  • Tracking & Third-Party Couriers: We cannot live-track or control a parcel once handed over to the courier (e.g., Lalamove, J&T Express, Flash Express, LBC). Any delivery status, delays, or rerouting are managed by the courier. Please refer to the courier’s app/website using the waybill we provide (when available), or contact the courier directly.
  • Same-day services (e.g., Lalamove) are subject to driver availability and real-time traffic; tracking and driver communication happen in the courier’s app.
  • Estimated delivery times shown at checkout are ETAs from the courier and may vary due to location, weather, or operational constraints.
  • Risk of loss passes upon delivery to your address or authorized representative. Ensure the address and contact details are accurate to avoid failed deliveries.

Inspect on Arrival

  • Please check your item upon delivery. For damaged/incorrect items, report within 48 hours with photos/unboxing video so we can assist quickly.

Returns & Exchanges

  • Allowed for manufacturing defects or wrong items received, in line with RA 7394. Items must be unused and in original packaging unless defective.
  • Consumables (e.g., water treatments, foods) and customized items are generally non-returnable unless defective.
  • Return shipping: shouldered by Chase Aquatics if our fault; otherwise by customer.

Warranty

  • Unless stated otherwise, non-consumable equipment carries a standard warranty from the manufacturer/distributor. We’ll help facilitate claims.
  • Warranty exclusions: misuse, improper installation, unauthorized modification, normal wear and tear, and damage from water-chemistry neglect.

Live/Fishable Items (if applicable)

  • Live Arrival Guarantee applies only if the delivery is received on the first attempt and acclimation instructions are followed. Claims must include clear photos within 1 hour of receipt.

Force Majeure

  • We’re not liable for delays or failures caused by events beyond our reasonable control (e.g., severe weather, natural disasters, carrier disruptions).

Governing Law

  • These terms are governed by Philippine law, including the Consumer Act (RA 7394) and E-Commerce Act (RA 8792). Venue may be the courts of your city or Taguig—customize as needed.
